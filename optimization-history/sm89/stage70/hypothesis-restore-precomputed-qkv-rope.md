# Stage 70: restore precomputed RoPE in the fused QKV epilogue

## Why this route is reopened

The clean post-Stage68 S2 checkpoint puts QKV+RoPE second at 25.98% busy union and 11.25%
exclusive busy. Broad NCU reports 30.528 us, 240 registers/thread, 49.152 KiB shared memory,
only 14.9% active warps, and 53.6% L2 throughput.

Stage 30 replaced per-output `__sincosf` with a model-lifetime float2 table. It was byte-identical,
improved three-launch NCU by 0.72%, and reduced the QKV family in both natural S1 Nsys orders by
about 1.1-1.5%. It was nevertheless deleted because it missed an obsolete 5% local threshold and
whole-forward S2-direction evidence was phase-sensitive. Under the current workflow, strictly
better NCU plus a strictly better natural local boundary is enough to retain a default-off
intrinsic optimization; S2 controls deployment, not source retention.

## Implementation and prediction

Reuse the already existing per-attention-block `ropeCosSinTable`, generated by the same GPU
`__sincosf` expression. Pass it into `Sm89QKVRoPEGemmB13` when
`cudaUsePrecomputedQKRoPESm89=true`. The Q/K epilogue loads `(cos,sin)` by `(xy,head,pair)`;
V and the null-table direct-recompute path are unchanged.

Predictions:

- 26-row output remains byte-identical;
- no launch is added to steady-state forward;
- QKV math-pipe pressure falls, while registers and L2 pressure may rise;
- same-kernel NCU and natural S1 QKV boundary must both be strictly lower to retain;
- short natural S2 decides only whether to deploy it now.

The old private-table implementation is restored first as a clean reproduction. Cross-stream
table sharing is a separate follow-up only if duplicated S2 table capacity is again identified as
the limiting mechanism.
